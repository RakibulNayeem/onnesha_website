What each role can do

Student

Practice, take tests, see their own progress. No panel access.

Teacher

Writes questions. Sees only their own; everything saves as a draft.

Moderator

Reviews and publishes everyone's questions; edits and deletes them.

Admin

Everything above plus content and users (up to Moderator).

Superuser

Everything, including creating Admins.
